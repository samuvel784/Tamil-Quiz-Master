# Privacy Policy — Tamil Quiz Master (தமிழ் வினாடி வினா)

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.tamilquizmaster.app`  
**Contact Email:** samuvel072@gmail.com  
**Target Audience:** Ages 13+  

---

## 1. App Summary
**Tamil Quiz Master** is an offline-first bilingual (Tamil & English) educational quiz and flashcard application covering Tamil grammar, literature, history, science, and general knowledge. The app operates without requiring user registration, login, or cloud accounts.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
We are committed to user privacy:
- We do **NOT** require or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or user accounts.
- We do **NOT** access or collect precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, or device storage files.
- We do **NOT** upload your quiz scores, bookmarks, or study activity to external servers.

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
To provide core quiz functionality, the following data is saved **exclusively on your device** using Android Room (SQLite) and Jetpack DataStore:
- **Quiz History & 7-Day Analytics:** Scores, question counts, accuracy percentages, XP, streaks, and attempt timestamps.
- **Bookmarks & Study Progress:** Questions you bookmark for review.
- **App Preferences:** Language selection (`ta` / `en`), countdown timer difficulty (`normal` / `challenging` / `blitz`), sound effects toggle, haptic vibration toggle, theme mode, and font scale.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Uses your device's built-in speech engine locally on-device to read questions aloud in Tamil. No audio recordings are collected or sent to servers.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Google AdMob Advertising
To keep educational features free, **Tamil Quiz Master** displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads served via **Google AdMob**.
- **AdMob Data Collection:** Google AdMob may collect and use the device advertising ID, IP address, device diagnostics, and interaction data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance in accordance with [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **User Consent & Privacy Settings:** Upon first launch, users are presented with a consent form managed by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to manage personalized vs. non-personalized ad consent choices. Users can change their ad consent preferences at any time by navigating to **Settings → Privacy settings** within the app.

## 5. Children's Privacy (Ages 13+)
**Tamil Quiz Master** is intended for a general educational audience aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. User Control & Data Deletion
- You can permanently erase all local quiz scores, XP, streaks, bookmarks, and analytics anytime via **Settings → Reset All Progress**.
- Uninstalling the application also permanently deletes all locally stored app data from your device.
