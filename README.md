# Tamil Quiz Master (தமிழ் வினாடி வினா)

**Developer:** Vurka Studio  
**Package ID:** `com.tamilquizmaster.app`  
**Target Audience:** Ages 13+  
**Architecture:** Offline-First Android App built with Jetpack Compose & Room Database.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**Tamil Quiz Master** developed by **Vurka Studio** is a rich, bilingual (Tamil & English) educational quiz application designed for students, competitive exam aspirants (TNPSC, NEET, Government exams), and Tamil literature enthusiasts. It offers over 1,000+ verified questions across 10 essential subject categories.

---

## 📚 Subject Categories
1. **Tamil Grammar & Language** (இலக்கணம் & மொழி)
2. **Literature & Epics** (இலக்கியம் & காப்பியம் - Thirukkural, Silappathikaram, etc.)
3. **Tamil History** (தமிழக வரலாறு - Chera, Chola, Pandya, Pallava)
4. **Tamil Nadu GK & Symbols** (தமிழ்நாடு GK & சின்னங்கள்)
5. **Science & Scholars** (அறிவியல் & அறிஞர்கள் - C.V. Raman, Abdul Kalam, ISRO)
6. **NEET / TNPSC Science** (அறிவியல் - Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
7. **Government Exam GK** (அரசுத் தேர்வு GK - Indian Polity & General Awareness)
8. **Math Aptitude** (கணிதத் திறன்)
9. **Culture & Proverbs** (பழமொழிகள் & மரபு)
10. **Game-Style Quizzes** (விளையாட்டு முறை வினாடி)

---

## 💎 Economy & Reward System
- **Diamonds (`💎`)**: The primary in-game reward currency earned via daily logins, completing quizzes, maintaining streaks, and optional rewarded video ads.
- **Hearts (`❤️`)**: Lives required during quiz play. Can be recovered via rewarded video ads or purchased in the Store using diamonds.
- **Hints (`💡`)**: Used during quizzes to eliminate incorrect answer options.
- **Mystery Box**: A special premium package available in the Store offering bonus hearts and hints.
- **Daily Login Rewards**: 7-day streak rewards for continuous daily engagement.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Compliance
- **Offline-First Storage**: All quiz scores, streaks, bookmarks, and user settings are stored **strictly on-device** using Android Room and DataStore. No personal data is uploaded to external servers.
- **Google AdMob & UMP Consent**: Complies with Google Play policies using the User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) for ad consent management.
- **Target Audience**: Designed for users aged **13 and older**.
