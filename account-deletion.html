Offline-First App (No User Accounts)

Tamil Quiz Master by Vurka Studio is an offline-first educational quiz application. We do not require user account registration, sign-in, or remote cloud storage of personal data. Therefore, no remote user accounts exist on external servers.

How to Delete Your Local Data

All your local quiz history, streaks, bookmarks, and app preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android Room and DataStore. You can permanently erase all your data instantly using either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Reset: Open the app, navigate to Settings → Reset All Progress, and confirm. This permanently clears all local database records.
  2. App Uninstall: Simply uninstall Tamil Quiz Master from your Android device. Android will automatically and permanently delete all local app data and storage associated with the app.
