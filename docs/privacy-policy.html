Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Developer: Vurka Studio • Target Audience: Ages 13+ • Contact: samuvel072@gmail.com

1. App Summary & Audience (13+)

Tamil Quiz Master (தமிழ் வினாடி வினா) by Vurka Studio is an offline-first bilingual educational quiz application designed for users aged 13 and older. No user account registration or login is required.

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone data.

3. Google AdMob & Advertising ID

To keep educational features free, the app displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads served via Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use the device advertising ID, IP address, and device information to serve ads and measure ad performance in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Users can manage ad consent choices or update preferences at any time via Settings → Privacy settings within the app.

4. Local On-Device Storage Only

Quiz scores, 7-day analytics, bookmarks, and app preferences are stored strictly on your device using Android Room and DataStore. You can permanently erase all local progress anytime via Settings → Reset All Progress or by uninstalling the app.

5. Text-to-Speech (TTS) Feature

The app includes an optional text-to-speech feature powered by your device's built-in speech engine to read questions aloud in Tamil. Audio processing is handled entirely on-device; no voice recordings are collected or transmitted.
